VARIANT A — VERTICAL AXIS OF ADJUDICATION.
The PRIMARY silhouette is VERTICAL, monumental and compact in horizontal length: a tall suspended tapered axis with height roughly 2.2 times its width and only moderate fore-aft depth. It should read as a moving, imposing engineered edifice in space, not an ocean ship turned upright. The outline is a broad elongated diamond with the top and bottom deliberately cut flat, not a needle or pointy tower. A single substantial dark central keel continues vertically. Four immense offset solid armor masses, two high and two low, hover a short controlled distance from the keel; each has deeply faceted thick cross-sections and clearly paired noncontact engineering faces. Their offsets are disciplined, making a broken but ordered vertical silhouette. Short dark structural bridges and protected mechanical galleries connect most of the central body; only two gaps are truly noncontact. The fore-facing side has a long extremely narrow illuminated incision in a flat physical boundary plane, embedded within the central keel and partly concealed by massive shoulder structures. No spherical core.

Authority is carried by the immutable tall center and four subordinate masses aligning to it. Several faint potential trajectories approach from varied directions, become briefly separated at the two controlled gaps, and align into ONE quiet forward vector. These lines must stay subtle and thin, never create a surrounding halo. The bow is the broad forward face, with very small protected propulsion apertures on the opposite aft faces visible in the three-quarter angle. Sixteen weapons: four emitter terraces integrated into the thickness of each of the four major armor masses. Their broad directional mouths point forward and remain secondary.

Composition: landscape 16:9, tall vessel centered with breathing room above and below, front three-quarter view with mild low elevation so the vertical authority is obvious while thickness and aft propulsion remain legible.
