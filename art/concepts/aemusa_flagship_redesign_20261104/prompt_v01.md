Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one original, breathtaking full-ship concept artwork for a unique human super-flagship in a Stellaris mod inspired by Aemusa's themes of probability, branching possibilities and chosen futures. This is a fresh complete redesign, not an edit of any earlier ship. Visual doctrine: "an engineered miracle": a modern post-disaster human civilization has integrated bounded extraordinary phenomena into advanced engineering.

Main design: a monumental BRAIDED, BRANCHING SPINE warship. Four enormous load-bearing paths divide from the heavily built aft engineering mass, pass around a deep, LONG central decision-volume in staggered vertical levels, then converge into a powerful broad, armored forward keel. The resulting silhouette has a deliberate irregular Y-shaped sequence of negative spaces, changing depth and interlocking terraces. The hollow central volume and the branching hull paths must be primary forms readable at thumbnail size, not a tiny gadget on a conventional flat battleship. Big connected architectural masses, deep undercuts and substantial volume; not a thin stick, flat aircraft carrier, flying playing card, crescent-wing ship or ringed starship. Keep a decisive forward direction and a clearly distinguishable propulsion aft. The silhouette must express multiple possible routes being held apart and brought together.

Weapon requirement: AT LEAST SIXTEEN true titanic-scale weapon stations, conceived as four armored fire-control/energy banks with four independently framed recessed emitters per bank. Show these as huge broad shallow directional emission faces integrated into stepped forward-facing shoulders, two banks at an upper level and two staggered lower outboard banks, each with substantial power and cooling volume behind it. Some far-side emitters may be occluded in perspective. These are separate large weapon stations, not decorative lights. No conventional gun barrels, naval gun turrets, repeated deck guns, long protruding cannon tubes, or sixteen tiny guns stuck to a hull. Weapons occupy major architectural volumes. Do not draw firing beams.

Aemusa identity: the main structural paths themselves fork and rejoin. In the open decision-volume, three to five bounded, discrete observation planes at slightly different inclinations are supported by paired field-control frames. They are integrated engineering interfaces, not giant literal tarot cards, not armor shingles, and not an omniscient magic shrine. Sparse nonclosed trajectory traces and low-intensity wine/rose readings locally reveal the choice of paths. No readable real-world tarot symbols or invented canon emblems.

Civilization material language: deep navy #182630 and graphite blue gray #304651 as the large armored masses, fog silver #9BA8AE for selected loaded joints and maintenance edges, restrained warm gray panels, cyan only for operational readings, tiny amber inhabited/maintenance windows. Local dark plum, deep wine and a little muted warm gold belong to the flagship's probability-control interfaces and a few principal pathway inlays; they must not repaint the whole hull. Show human-scale servicing, modular armor, docking recesses, substantial protected engine clusters and functional layered radiators. Awe comes from proportions, depth, mass and unusual structural organization. Avoid random greeble confetti, gold-white divine palaces, purple crystals, gothic churches, giant concentric rings or free-floating debris. Do not reuse any existing franchise's recognizable warship silhouette.

Composition: cinematic but highly readable professional spaceship concept painting, near-photoreal physically plausible hard-surface materials. Entire ship fully visible with margin, front three-quarter elevated view showing the deep central space and multiple levels, forward end toward lower left, aft receding upper right. A very small conventional escort silhouette nearby is only a scale cue, not another featured design. Dark quiet deep-space backdrop, soft directional neutral starlight and restrained rim light expose the form, no planet blocking the hull, no explosion, no excessive bloom. The ship should feel overwhelmingly more massive than an ordinary titan-scale warship without using a celestial body as its shell. Crisp major silhouettes and selective engineering detail, production concept quality rather than toy graybox. Landscape 16:9. No text, labels, logo, watermark or diagram overlay.
