# 七都美术风格校准稿提示词

工具：内置image_gen；全新生成，无图片输入。A／B／C及A2不作为本稿底图；至少16T继续有效，数目与实际接口另行实现。

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a new visual-style calibration concept for an original enormous unique spaceship, the Destiny Sovereign, in a Stellaris mod based on the modern urban-fantasy sensibility of Forever 7 Days (永远的7日之都). The user rejected prior ships because they looked like generic realistic military science fiction. The decisive change now is ART DIRECTION AND FORM LANGUAGE, not adding ornaments to an existing battleship.

Use the refined illustrated look seen across modern Japanese urban-fantasy game character props, artifact designs and 2.5D environments: confident clear contours, selectively simplified details, designed silhouettes, broad clean color masses, two to three deliberate shadow-value families, soft painted transitions only where useful, crisp economical highlights, and a harmonious relationship between geometric structure and graceful long curves. The finish should resemble an excellent ORIGINAL hand-painted game artifact/vehicle concept. No photographic PBR rendering, grit, dense scratches, noisy armor tiling, every-edge bevel glint, tiny mechanical greeble texture or movie-VFX spaceship realism. Do not merely apply a cartoon filter to a conventional spaceship.

Project setting: 《神器使》Stellaris Mod 的统一公共风格为灾后现代幻想星海文明。经历黑门危机与七日轮回、在中央庭治理下重建的人类将神器现象纳入科学研究。现代工程、清楚接口和可维护性构成实体基础；受控悬浮、空间错位和高维信息构成超常层。保持人性与日系幻想设计的清晰表达，理性、秩序、精密与克制的希望。此对象是唯一专属旗舰，允许鲜明独立的器物造型；不是普通公共设备的放大版。

New design: a single coherent, elegantly intimidating mobile vessel, with a compact long faceted core and THREE large sweeping structural shells around it. Their arrangement is deliberately asymmetric and diagonal, with one long high shell arching aft and two shorter lower shells cradling the keel. These forms have thick sculpted volume, refined taper, broad surface planes and flowing cutout edges, like an extraordinarily sophisticated artifact translated into monumental engineering. They are NOT literal feathers, cards, a dress, a sword, a ring, a crown or a character silhouette. The three shells are structural hull volumes, with protected equipment inside and only sparse visible joints. Clear prow pointing down-left, compact propulsion at the upper-right aft. Avoid twin-jaw/open-trench layouts, vertical skyscrapers and four-block weapons platforms.

Theme: mysterious, extraordinary, authoritative. One small deeply recessed central discontinuity appears as an impossibly dark, flat slice held between paired pale structural faces. A single thin pale-gold line continues steadily through it; two faint alternate branches end before the slice. ONE major outer shell has a precise stepped offset in its continuity, disciplined by visible opposing interfaces; all other connections remain physical. The whole balanced but nonuniform composition suggests that alternatives are organized around a chosen, unchanging course. This is only visual interpretation of the theme, not a statement of new gameplay powers. Let the solid silhouette carry the authority.

Color design: approximately half the visible hull in clean deep indigo/navy and blue-gray masses, balanced by substantial fog-silver and pale cool-gray surfaces. Use cool lavender-blue shadow color instead of crushed black and many gray metallic facets. Very small restrained wine/plum inner accents, tiny muted-gold functional edges, one concentrated pale-cyan technical focal point. Colors should remain distinct at thumbnail size. Materials are illustrated ceramic-composite shells, satin surfaces and selective delicate translucent light; they retain volume without photoreal surface noise. Do not copy any character's costume or any existing artifact silhouette.

Weapons and scale: reserve sixteen independent titanic weapon positions, eight per flank, in narrow sheltered apertures integrated below the sweeping shells. The weapon housings have solid volume but do not dominate the image as deck guns or window blocks. A few maintenance recesses and a tiny generic escort establish human scale. Quiet propulsion slots aft. No weapon firing. This is an art-direction study; it is not a count-certified technical blueprint.

Composition: one complete vessel only, professional illustrated three-quarter concept view, entire ship visible with generous margin, landscape 16:9. Background a clean pale blue-gray studio/concept-sheet wash with a restrained soft shape shadow; no busy environment and no dark space grading that hides palette. Fine controlled outlines, painted form shadows, readable negative space. No labels, text, watermark, diagram arrows, logos, UI panels or inset images. The final image should feel authored as a cohesive modern fantasy game object, beautiful and commanding, with clear direction and engineered mass.

Public exclusions: no generic medieval temple or pure fantasy shrine, no all-gold-white god ship, no purple crystal default, no copied recognizable franchise vessel, no limitless floating shards or concentric rings, no crowd of magic circles, no invented canon emblems. Absolutely no tarot cards, card-like floating screens or literal character accessories. Do not imitate any earlier generated ship.
